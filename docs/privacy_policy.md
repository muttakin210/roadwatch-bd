# Privacy policy — DRAFT, not for publication

> Needs review against current Bangladeshi data-protection law before beta (PLAN §8.6).
> This draft only lists what the system actually does, so the final text can be accurate.

- **What we collect:** the video you upload, optional location (map pin or the file's GPS), the camera type
  and direction arrows you draw, and your ✔/✖ feedback. No account is needed.
- **Blurring:** faces and number plates are blurred in everything that is shown, shared or downloaded.
  Only your private raw upload is unblurred.
- **Retention:** the raw video is deleted after **30 days** unless you tick the training-consent box.
  Results stay until you delete them. Feedback contains no video and is kept.
- **Training use:** only with your consent; only moderator-confirmed labels are used.
- **Wording:** results are "possible incidents (AI-detected, may be wrong)". We never name people or show plates.
- **Takedown:** use the report button on any result.
